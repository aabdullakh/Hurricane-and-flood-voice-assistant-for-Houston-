        How this works

        Where the data comes from

        • USGS stream gauges in Harris County: water level, read about every 15 minutes and sent about every hour, so readings can lag.
        • National Weather Service active alerts, such as flood watches and warnings.
        • US Census address lookup. ZIP codes and place names may use Zippopotam.us or OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
        • Open-Meteo rain estimates from a weather model, not a rain gauge.
        • OpenStreetMap map images, loaded through our server.
        • FEMA shelters reported open through FEMA's National Shelter System.
        • ElevenLabs the voice assistant, which uses the same live data.

        What it can't do

        • This is a prototype built for a hackathon. It is not an official warning service.
        • Risk comes from official alerts and how fast gauges are rising. We don't have official flood levels for each gauge.
        • A gauge measures water at the gauge, not on your street. A spot more than 10 miles from a gauge shows as unknown.
        • It never tells you a place is safe. Never drive through water: turn around, don't drown.

        For official information: ReadyHarris, Houston Office of Emergency Management, Harris County Flood Warning System, National Weather Service Houston, or call 211.

        Privacy

        • Voice calls are processed by ElevenLabs. Your conversation may be recorded and stored by ElevenLabs.
        • Don't share personal information (like your full home address or phone number) unless you need to.
        • Our own server does not store call audio or full transcripts. If you say yes when the assistant asks, it saves a few household details (like your first name, language and needs) to help next time. Ask it to forget you at any time. We may keep a short summary of what a call was about.
        • Map points you check are rounded to about 1 km before they reach our server. Our server keeps short technical logs (such as IP address and time) that are deleted automatically.
        • This website sets no cookies and has no ads, analytics or tracking. The voice assistant may save small settings in your browser, such as your language.

        Prototype decision support, not official guidance. Data may be delayed or incomplete. In immediate danger, call 911.
